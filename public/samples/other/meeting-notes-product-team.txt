Product Team Meeting Notes - March 10

Agenda:
1. Review progress on document indexing system
2. Improve search performance
3. Discuss UI improvements for file preview

Discussion summary:
The engineering team reported that search indexing is now able to
process PDF, DOCX, and TXT files. The next step is implementing
keyword highlighting in search results.

The design team suggested adding a preview panel so users can
quickly read documents before downloading them.
