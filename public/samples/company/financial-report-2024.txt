Financial Report 2024

The company reported a total revenue growth of 18% compared to the previous fiscal year.
Cloud infrastructure services and AI consulting contributed significantly to this growth.

Operating costs were reduced through improved server utilization and migration to a more
efficient container-based architecture.

Key highlights:
- Revenue growth driven by enterprise software subscriptions
- Increased demand for AI automation tools
- Expansion into Southeast Asian markets

The company plans to invest further in machine learning infrastructure
and developer productivity tools in 2025.
